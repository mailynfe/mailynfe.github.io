Appetizers

Onigiri

Featured in Fruits Basket

Onigiri are Japanese rice balls made from steamed rice and often filled with ingredients such as salmon, tuna, or pickled plum. They are commonly wrapped in nori seaweed and make a delicious portable snack.

Karaage

Featured in Isekai Izakaya

Karaage is a popular Japanese fried chicken dish. Bite-sized pieces of chicken are marinated in soy sauce, ginger, and garlic before being coated and fried until golden brown and crispy.

Katsu Sandwich

Featured in Restaurant from Another World

A katsu sandwich is a Japanese sandwich made with a crispy breaded cutlet placed between slices of soft bread. It is often served with shredded cabbage and a rich tonkatsu sauce.

Main Dishes

Omurice

Featured in Blend S

Omurice is a Japanese comfort food made with seasoned fried rice wrapped inside a soft omelet. It is commonly topped with ketchup or a savory sauce.

Japanese Curry

Featured in Anime Name Here

Japanese curry is a comforting dish made with potatoes, carrots, onions, and your choice of protein. The ingredients are simmered in a thick, flavorful curry sauce and served over steamed rice.

Gyudon

Featured in Demon Slayer: Mugen Train

Gyudon is a Japanese rice bowl topped with thinly sliced beef and onions simmered in a sweet and savory sauce. It is a filling and flavorful meal.

Desserts

Kiki's Chocolate Cake

Featured in Kiki's Delivery Service

Inspired by the famous cake from Kiki's Delivery Service, this rich chocolate cake is decorated with a design inspired by the movie. It is a fun dessert for anime fans.

Mango Parfait

Featured in Kaichou wa Maid-sama

Mango parfait is a refreshing dessert made with layers of sweet mango, whipped cream, and other delicious toppings. It is colorful, sweet, and perfect for dessert lovers.

Canelés

Featured in Your Lie in April

Canelés are small French pastries known for their caramelized crust and soft, custard-like center. They are traditionally flavored with vanilla and rum.

Strawberry Shortcake

Featured in Ouran High School Host Club

Japanese strawberry shortcake is a light and fluffy sponge cake layered with fresh strawberries and whipped cream. It is a popular dessert for celebrations.
