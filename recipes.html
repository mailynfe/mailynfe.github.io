Explore recipes inspired by delicious dishes featured throughout the world of anime. Browse appetizers, main dishes, and desserts to find your next recipe to try.

Appetizers

Onigiri

Featured in: Fruits Basket

Onigiri are Japanese rice balls that can be filled with ingredients such as salmon, tuna, or pickled plum. They are often wrapped or decorated with nori and make a simple and portable snack.

Onigiri Photo

Karaage

Featured in: Isekai Izakaya

Karaage is Japanese-style fried chicken. Pieces of chicken are marinated, coated, and fried until they are crispy on the outside and juicy on the inside.

Karaage Photo

Katsu Sandwich

Featured in: Anime Name Here

A katsu sandwich features crispy breaded cutlet layered between slices of soft bread. It is often paired with a flavorful sauce and shredded cabbage.

Katsu Sandwich Photo

Main Dishes

Omurice

Featured in: Blend S

Omurice is a Japanese comfort food made with seasoned fried rice wrapped in a soft omelet. It is commonly finished with ketchup or another flavorful sauce.

Omurice Photo

Japanese Curry

Featured in: Restaurant to Another World

Japanese curry is a hearty dish made with a rich curry sauce, vegetables, and often meat. It is traditionally served over steamed rice.

Curry Photo

Gyudon

Featured in: Demon Slayer: Mugen Train

Gyudon is a Japanese rice bowl topped with thinly sliced beef and onions simmered in a sweet and savory sauce. It is a simple and filling meal.

Gyudon Photo

Neapolitan

Featured in: Isekai Izakaya

Neapolitan is a Japanese-style spaghetti dish made with pasta, tomato-based sauce, and ingredients such as onions, peppers, mushrooms, and sausage.
